## Market Sentiment & The Year Ahead

Broadly speaking - 3 themes emerged from the sales meeting. Bear in mind this discussion was about *all* asset classes, not just multifamily which is among the most resilient (retail, development land, office, industrial etc.). 

**1/ Insolvency's Abound for Residential Land** 

Our development teams have been seeing a TON of receivership activity. Insolvency Insider captures this well. Lender patience is waning in the residential land sector. The year may start with significant insolvency activity but could slow down later. Real estate, construction services, traditional retail, and hospitality are expected to face significant stress.

**2/ Cautious Optimism for 2024**

Looking at 2023 - activity was down considerably in all asset classes. To use Toronto multifamily as an example - sales were down 51.2% in 2023 year-over-year (volume decreased from $2.3B in 2022 to $1.1Bn in 2023).

Rising interest rates put many large institutional players on defence. Now - with inflation seemingly tempered and both the BoC and the Fed set to lower rates - most feel activity will (gradually) resume.

**3/ Trends From 2023 Continue**

In 2023 we saw several trends emerge. Vendor financing became more common, portfolios were split up to be sold, private buyers and non-profits became a bigger percentage of the buyer pool, and new builds were a hot commodity. Many of these trends will continue and become more pronounced in 2024.

**Closing Thoughts**

There is no consensus on exactly where the market is. The bulls argue that we hit the floor already, and as rates decrease activity and cap rates will start returning to their 2021 levels. The bears would argue that cap rate have not climbed nearly enough to compensate for where interest rates will land. They predict a much more tempered path forward.

With that in mind - now is the time for a lot of conversations. For sellers looking for the right time - test the waters. For purchasers unsure of where cap rates will settle - throw a ton of offers. In this market - activity will pay off.
