October 13th, 2023

**Read Time:** 2 minutes

Spooky stats to share this Friday the 13th.

Today we release our Q3 report for the GTA multifamily market (download below).

Sales volume is down 85.4% YoY. Needless to say, interest rates are having an effect on our multifamily market.

Four takeaways for you to chew on:

*(did someone forward you this email? Sign up **[here](http://www.onmultifamily.com)**).*

**1. Toronto is Extreme**

Value-add properties in Toronto have been materially impacted by the combination of rising rates and slowing turnover.

It's tough to buy a 3 cap at 5% financing when you can't increase rents.

Our other Ontario markets are fairing a little better.

**2. Volatility in Rates**

It's not the increase in rates - it's the volatility.

Rates are jumping and falling 25 bps a day.

Very challenging for buyers to waive conditions without a grasp of where their financing will land.

Once rates stabilize - you can anticipate more activity.

**3. Q4 is always a Monster**

For a number of reasons - yearends, bonuses, taxes etc. Q4 is consistently a big quarter.

We're predicting a slight reversion to the mean over the next few months.
