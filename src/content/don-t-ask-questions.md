One of the best books I read last year was The Truth Detector.

In it - ex-FBI Agent, Dr. Jack Schafer details strategies he used to gather counterintelligence - on murderers, white supremacists etc. 

You can use those same strategies to ellicate the truth when you're doing deals.

The main rule - don't ask questions.

Instead, use presumptive statements.

**A presumptive statement** is a technique where you make an assumption or present a statement that presumes specific information. The goal is to prompt the other person to correct, confirm, or expand upon the statement, thereby revealing more information than they might have intended. It’s a subtle way of gathering information without directly asking questions.

**Contrast with Asking Questions:** When you ask direct questions, the other party is fully aware that you are seeking specific information. It can make them more guarded and less likely to share openly. In contrast, a presumptive statement is less intrusive. It feels like a part of the natural flow of conversation, making the other person more comfortable and likely to reveal information.

For instance - if you ask, "What price do you need to hit to sell your building?"

The owner will respond with: 

A) "I'm not telling you that" or 

B) A lie (i.e. an unreasonably high price you know they've made up).

Instead, you use a presumptive statement.

"I heard you guys aren't willing to sell unless we get you $15M".

Owner's response:

"No! We need at least $16M to consider a sale."

Much more helpful.

Another Example:

Conventional method (asking the question):

"Hey Buyer - how much more room do you have in your offer?" 

Response: "Not much" (guarded).

Instead - use a presumptive statement.

"You guys assembled the other two properties and this is the key piece. On a land residual you guys can pay $15M for this".

Response: "No, we're still working on the second owner, and we can't pay more than $14.5M"

Better. 

Now I've obviously simplified things here. But honestly - I use this technique all the time to help my clients win.

You can use it to uncover potential risks with a property, seller motivations, buyer and seller strike price and anything else where someone would otherwise hold their cards close to their chest.

Hope you find it helpful.
