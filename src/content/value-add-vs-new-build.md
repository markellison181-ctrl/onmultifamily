**New vs. Value-Add Multifamily in Ontario: Two Strategies, One Big Decision**

The Ontario multifamily market is buzzing with opportunity—but which path should you take?

On one hand, sleek, modern buildings offer stability and ease. On the other, older properties are value-add playgrounds with HUGE upside in rents.

Both approaches can lead to success, but each comes with its own pros, cons, and risks.

Whether you’re building long-term wealth or chasing aggressive returns, understanding the key differences is critical.

# **New Multifamily Properties**

*Turnkey investments designed for long-term stability and minimal headaches.*

**Why New Properties Shine:**

**Freedom from Rent Control:** Properties first occupied after November 15, 2018, allow landlords to set market rents without annual caps.

- **High Efficiency, Low Costs:** New buildings feature energy-saving HVAC systems, smart appliances, and modern insulation, cutting operational expenses.

- **Attract Premium Tenants:** Higher-income tenants are drawn to sleek designs, top-tier amenities, and the convenience of new builds.

- **Lower Insurance Costs:** Newer buildings mean less risk, resulting in lower insurance premiums.

- **Tenant-Paid Utilities:** Separate metering is common, allowing tenants to shoulder utility costs while improving your NOI.

- **Modern Curb Appeal:** New properties make strong impressions—helping with leasing, refinancing, and eventual resale.

- **ESG-Friendly Designs:** Sustainability is increasingly attractive to both tenants and institutional investors.

- **Below Replacement Cost Deals:** Sometimes, you can buy for less than it would cost to build new.

**The Trade-Offs:**

- **High Entry Costs:** Expensive per-unit prices make scaling portfolios a challenge.

- **Lower Initial Yields:** Higher purchase prices mean modest early returns.

- **Lease-Up Risks:** New buildings in competitive markets may take longer to fill.

- **Policy Uncertainty:** Rent control exemptions could change, reshaping the financial picture.

- **Tight Inventory:** Developers often hold long-term, so finding these gems can be tough.

# **Value-Add Multifamily Properties**

*The sweat-equity option for those willing to create value through effort.*

**Why Value-Add Properties Win:**

- **Affordable Entry Point:** Lower per-unit prices allow easier entry, even for first-time investors.

- **Higher Yields:** Better cash-on-cash returns during term of the investment.

- **Reposition for Profit:** Renovate, rebrand, and reposition to boost rents and unlock significant equity gains.

- **Stable Income:** Many older buildings enjoy low vacancy rates with long-term tenants providing steady cash flow.

- **Tax Benefits:** Depreciation, renovation write-offs, and interest expenses offer meaningful deductions.

- **Less Competition:** While many chase new builds, older properties can be hidden gems.

- **Prime Locations:** Many value-add properties sit in established neighbourhoods with strong demand.

**The Challenges:**

- **Deferred Maintenance Costs:** Aging HVAC, plumbing, or roofs often require costly repairs.

- **Rent Control Caps:** Older buildings are subject to Ontario’s annual rent increase limits and lower turnover.

- **Higher Operating Costs:** Without separate meters, landlords may bear the brunt of utility expenses.

- **Management Intensive:** Value-add projects require active involvement to oversee upgrades and tenant relations.

**Exit Strategy:**

- **New Builds:** Due to the high per unit cost and lower, but stable returns - large institutions / scaled asset managers are the primary buyers. Scalable, ESG friendly, and perfect for the cover of the investment letter. 

- **Value-Add Properties:** This category attracts every buyer profile - from large institutions to mom and pop local investors. Value-add is attractive to everyone.

**TL;DR**

- **New Builds:** Perfect for institutional and private groups prioritizing stability and ESG. High acquisition costs but minimal maintenance. Freedom from rent control means potential for predictable returns and long-term growth.

- **Value-Add Properties:** Accessible to everyone. Lower upfront costs with high upside through renovations and rent increases. More hands-on but often a faster way to build equity.
