## A quick RTA update

Three changes that took effect yesterday, September 21, should help owners address unpaid and persistently late rent:

- **N4s:** For new unpaid-rent notices, the minimum notice period is now seven days rather than 14, allowing owners to file an LTB application sooner if rent remains unpaid.

- **Arrears hearings:** For applications filed from September 21, tenants generally must pay the landlord at least half the claimed arrears at least seven days before the hearing to raise additional issues.

- **Persistent late payment:** Rent received more than seven days late, at least three times in six months, now expressly meets this test, subject to the rules on how payments are applied.

N12 compensation and renovation return-notice requirements also changed. Have your manager or paralegal review the [full LTB update and forms](https://tribunalsontario.ca/2026/09/21/ltb-operational-update-legislative-changes-at-the-landlord-and-tenant-board-effective-september-21-2026/).

## Where the market stands

The recent increase in bond yields has created some apprehension, particularly among buyers working through financing. The five-year CMB is approximately 3.70% this morning, and the speed of the increase has caught some people off guard. That said, financing remains in a relatively good position compared with fall 2023, when the same benchmark was approaching 4.8%.

We continue to see strong interest and competition for the right opportunities. Bid depth is property-dependent, but new buyers are coming to the table across different asset types. The breadth of that interest remains encouraging.

Market rents are also moderating. Many of our clients are reporting an uptick in interest from prospective tenants versus a few months prior.

The federal government's [push to attract infrastructure capital and improve incentives for business investment](https://www.pm.gc.ca/en/news/news-releases/2026/09/15/first-canada-investment-summit-unleashes-nearly-500-billion-new) is also encouraging. For apartment owners, I see the longer-term benefit in where those commitments translate into projects, employment and rental demand.
