CAPREIT's leadership did a tremendous job on the earnings call.

Below are our team's notes with reference to Mark Kenney and Julian Schonfeldt (CAPREIT's CEO and CIO respectively).

**Market Outlook: Navigating Future Valuations Amid Interest Rate Shifts:**

- Mark Kenney's Market Analysis: Kenney provides a reassuring outlook on property values, attributing stability to easing bond yields and a potential end to the tightening cycle: "the tightening cycle is at least plateauing at this point and not expected to get much worse."

- Julian Schonfeldt on Rate Impacts: Schonfeldt agrees, suggesting cap rates are nearing a high point, with promising rent growth offering a buffer for future property values: "the cap rate expansion is... potentially at its peak."

**Investment Landscape: Changes in Competition and Acquisition Opportunities:**

- Julian Schonfeldt on Acquisition Trends: He notes a shift to a less competitive environment for acquisitions, with current high bond yields and capital scarcity: "it's not anywhere near as competitive."

- Mark Kenney Predicts Institutional Movement: Looking ahead, Kenney anticipates increased trading activity by institutions in response to monetary policy and market rates by the next year: "A real pick up probably Q2Q3 of next year."

**Rental Market Analysis: Assessing Stability and Regional Dynamics:**

- Mark Kenney Reports on Rental Trends: He points to stable conditions in the rental market, with specific challenges in Ontario's market due to its low turnover rates linked to housing affordability: "The strongest market in Canada is clearly Ontario."

**Strategic Acquisitions: Capitalizing on Current Market Pressures:**

- Julian Schonfeldt's Approach to Opportunity: Schonfeldt highlights the company's strategic position to take advantage of the current market, with its robust financial standing and agility: "we are able to act quick."

- Mark Kenney on Building a Strong Reputation: Kenney underscores the company's longstanding reputation as a significant strategic asset in facilitating rapid and successful acquisitions.

**Operational Strategy: Pivoting Towards Efficiency and Value:**

- Mark Kenney Discusses Strategy Shift: Kenney speaks to the company's transition from focusing on growth by numbers to enhancing operational efficiency and quality of assets.

- Looking Ahead with CAPREIT 2.0: The company aims to rebalance its portfolio by trading high-value assets for those with better long-term prospects, marking a strategic evolution into what Kenney calls "CAPREIT 2.0."
