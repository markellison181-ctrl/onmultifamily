# Insurance Market Softens

A friend of mine, responsible for managing costs across a 4,000-unit portfolio, recently mentioned that he’s been able to negotiate better premiums on several of his buildings. Intrigued, our team investigated further, and here’s what we discovered.

The Hard Market (2019-2021)

A hard insurance market occurs when insurers raise premiums, reduce coverage options, and enforce stricter underwriting. This typically follows significant losses or low investment returns, as was the case between 2019 and 2021. During this period, insurers were hit by substantial claims from natural disasters and faced lower returns due to declining interest rates. These factors led to some of the steepest insurance premium increases in North America over the past decade.

Impact on Multifamily Owners in Ontario

**Increased Costs:** Multifamily property owners in Ontario saw insurance premiums skyrocket, severely impacting their operational budgets.

**Stricter Underwriting:** Insurance providers became more selective, making it harder for owners to secure comprehensive coverage. Many were left with less favourable policies or were forced to accept higher deductibles.

# Fast Forward to Today

In 2024, old insurance companies have re-entered the market, and competition has driven better pricing. For many, insurance premiums are no longer on the rise.

To put this into perspective:

In Q4 2020, the median change in insurance costs was a staggering 21.6% increase year-over-year. Today, that number has dropped to just a 7.2% increase, with 25% of policyholders seeing no rate increase at all.

Clients are reporting more competitive insurance bids and, in some cases, a reduction in premiums. At scale, these savings can be significant.
